Common questions

What counts as a developer?

Anyone who opens pull requests that Korekt reviews. Stakeholders, PMs, and read-only users don't count.

Is there a minimum team size?

No. Works for teams of 2 or 200. Pricing stays the same per developer.

Do you offer annual billing?

Yes. Annual billing gives you 2 months free — effectively $20/developer/month.

Can I try it first?

Book a demo and we'll set up a pilot on your actual repos. You see real results before committing.

How does the on-prem plan work?

We deploy Korekt directly into your infrastructure — your code never leaves your network. Pricing is tailored to your team size and requirements. Reach out to our sales team to get started.
